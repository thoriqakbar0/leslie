# Leslie launch

## Ready-to-post announcement

Leslie v0.2.1 is available as a public macOS beta.

**The to-do list you’ll actually keep.**

Plans change. Work appears that was never on the list. A normal task list keeps the original
promise and asks you to review what remains.

Leslie keeps planned work beside a factual record of what you actually did, so you can return to today
without rebuilding the week.

Plan tasks and estimates. Log work that appeared. Keep notes, folders, labels, and activity history.
Leslie stores its data on your Mac, with no account or cloud sync.

The source is public under MIT, so you can inspect it, change it, or maintain your own version.

Download the beta: https://leslie.ta-0.com/

This build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Right-click Leslie and choose **Open** on the first launch
if macOS asks.
